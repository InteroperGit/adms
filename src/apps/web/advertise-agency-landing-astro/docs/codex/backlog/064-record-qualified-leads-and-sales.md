# Record qualified leads and resulting sales

**Status:** Backlog — pending

**Moved to backlog:** 2026-10-10, at user request

**Priority:** Measurement

**Created:** 2026-10-09

**Plan:** [Sales and SEO improvement plan](../plan/sales-and-seo-plan.md).

**Depends on:** Tasks 051 and 063; agency owner and approved operational workflow.

## Goal

Connect inquiries to lead quality and actual sales outcomes.

## Scope

- Agree definitions for received inquiry, qualified lead, quoted lead, won sale,
  and lost opportunity, including how repeat contacts are deduplicated.
- Use an agreed lightweight register or existing CRM; document fields for
  inquiry reference, date, source/service, qualification, outcome, and value
  where available, with access/retention following task 050.
- Assign an agency owner and update cadence; retain attribution across phone
  follow-ups and orders without sending personal data to public analytics.
- Walk an authorized controlled example through receipt, qualification, and
  outcome reporting; explain source gaps and manual attribution limits.

## Acceptance

- The agency has a documented usable workflow from received inquiry to sale,
  with responsible owner and consistent status definitions.
- A controlled example links to its source/service without counting phone
  clicks, mock deliveries, duplicate inquiries, or quotes as sales.
