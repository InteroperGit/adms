## Goal

Keep the agency's business details consistent across local discovery sources.

## Scope

- Inventory existing agency business/map listings and compare name, address,
  phone, hours, service area, website URL, and supplied service descriptions.
- Align website contact/footer/legal data with the approved task 050 record;
  document legitimate differences such as a separate inquiry mailbox.
- Prepare exact listing corrections for agency review and apply only through
  authorized accounts/actions; record source URLs, change dates, and owners.
- Verify public details and website/phone/map destinations after corrections;
  document inaccessible accounts or pending external moderation.

## Acceptance

- Website details agree with approved agency facts, and listing discrepancies
  are corrected or tracked with a responsible owner.
- External listing publication is not inferred from a prepared draft; no new
  business identity, address, or service claim is invented.
